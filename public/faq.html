Frequently asked questions

Important details, plainly stated.

What equipment is currently in scope?

53-foot Dry Van is within the primary written scope. Reefer coordination requires a signed amendment, per-truck review, written authorization, and capacity confirmation; it does not include temperature monitoring or a temperature guarantee.

Who decides whether to take a load?

The carrier holds 100% decision authority. Almajdoub Dispatch negotiates and presents the details, but never accepts a load or signs a Rate Confirmation on your behalf without your direct approval.

What is your policy on Detention and TONU fees?

100% of negotiated Detention ($50/hr standard), Layover, and TONU payments are passed directly to the carrier settlement. We charge 0% commission on accessorials.

Does joining the Founder Waitlist guarantee service?

No. Registration records founder-seat eligibility at the time accepted. Carrier and each requested truck are still reviewed manually against eligibility and actual capacity.

When does the Founder Waitlist close?

After five distinct carriers complete an eligible first invoice that has been paid and reconciled. Waitlist registrations, approvals, trials, and unpaid invoices do not count. There is no calendar deadline.

Can I sign up for a trial or referral reward?

Yes. Review our structured 14-Day Trial Guidelines for evaluation terms, or explore our Carrier Referral Program to earn 14-day cumulative dispatch credits upon verified peer enrollment.

How do I send a privacy request?

Email contact@almajdoubdispatch.com. Do not attach tax, driver, insurance, banking, load, or location documents.
