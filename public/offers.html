Break-Even Decision Math

Which model puts more money in your pocket?

Choose based on your average weekly linehaul. No guesswork.

BELOW $4,271 / WEEK

Standard 7% Wins

If your 53' Dry Van gross linehaul is under $4,271/week, paying 7% per load costs less than a flat fee. For example, $3,500 gross = $245 dispatch fee.

ABOVE $4,271 / WEEK

$299 Flat Rate Wins

Once your truck gross exceeds $4,271/week, the $299 Flat Rate saves you money on every mile. A $6,000 gross week costs only $299 (effective 4.98%), saving you $121/week.
