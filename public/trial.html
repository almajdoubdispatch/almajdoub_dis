14 calendar days · intended eligibility

Trial terms need to be settled in writing first.

The owner-directed trial is intended for a directly registered, non-referred carrier with one accepted truck on the percentage plan. The 14-calendar-day period starts at administrative approval.

Intended to qualifyOne accepted truck; percentage plan; direct application; no valid referral attribution; administrative approval.
ExcludedReferred applicants and Flat Rate enrollments are not eligible for the ordinary 14-day trial.
Owner intentNo dispatch service fee during the trial. This intent is not yet aligned in Schedule A.
Not automaticTrial expiry does not create a paid plan or authorize a charge. A paid plan must be selected and accepted in writing.

Why this page is held: the existing Schedule A does not fully resolve the fee treatment for loads coordinated during the trial and all related billing terms. There is no live trial application or activation here. The trial must not be advertised as “free” or started until the written terms are aligned and reviewed.
