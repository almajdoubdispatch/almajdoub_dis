Carrier Referral & Direct Enrollment

Refer Fellow Carriers. Earn 14-Day Dispatch Credits.

When you recommend an active, safety-focused motor carrier to Almajdoub Dispatch, both carriers benefit under structured, written partnership terms.

The 14-Day Credit Incentive Mechanism

14-Day Service Fee Credit For each referred carrier that qualifies, the referring carrier receives a cumulative 14-calendar-day dispatch-fee credit per approved truck.
Earned on Conversion Only Credits are awarded only after the referred carrier completes and reconciles their first paid dispatch invoice. No credits for mere signups or unverified inquiries.
Flat Rate Waitlist Bypass Motor carriers enrolling directly under the Flat Rate weekly program ($245 Founder / $299 Standard) bypass the generic waitlist for expedited compliance review.
Transparent Accounting Credits are tracked and visible within your carrier billing statements. No cash disbursements or hidden rollover expirations.

How to Refer a Carrier

  1. Share your Carrier Legal Name or custom referral link with an owner-operator running 53' Dry Van or Reefer equipment.
  2. Your referred partner enters your company name in the referral field during digital onboarding.
  3. Our compliance team approves their authority, signs the dispatch agreement, and coordinates their first load.
  4. Upon acceptable POD delivery and first invoice reconciliation, your 14-day credit is immediately credited to your account.
