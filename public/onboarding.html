SUMMARY OF AGREEMENT TERMS & OPERATING BOUNDARIES (v2026-11-06)

1. APPOINTMENT & INDEPENDENCE: Carrier appoints Almajdoub Dispatch as independent dispatch coordinator. Almajdoub Dispatch is not a motor carrier or freight broker.

2. CARRIER CONTROL & SIGNATURE: Carrier retains 100% sole discretion to accept or decline any proposed shipment. Almajdoub Dispatch shall never accept a load without explicit carrier approval. Carrier signs its own Rate Confirmation directly with licensed brokers.

3. LIMITED POWER OF ATTORNEY: Carrier grants limited authority solely to contact licensed brokers, submit carrier setup packets, request certificates of insurance, and coordinate pickup/delivery routing instructions.

4. DISPATCH FEES & BILLING: Fees are calculated strictly on Linehaul as specified in Schedule A (e.g. 5% Founder / 7% Standard). Invoices are issued weekly on Friday following successful delivery and acceptable POD. Payment is due in accordance with Schedule A.

5. SEPARATION OF FUNDS: Carrier freight revenue is paid directly by brokers or factoring companies to Carrier. Almajdoub Dispatch never holds, factors, or commingles freight monies.
