Carrier Evaluation Program · Conditional Policy

14-Day Trial Eligibility & Operating Guidelines

We believe in mutual operational fit before long-term commitments. Our 14-calendar-day trial allows qualifying independent carriers to evaluate our dispatch coordination under defined contractual guidelines.

Qualifying Terms & Scope

Direct Percentage Applicants Only The evaluation period is reserved exclusively for direct percentage-plan enrollments running 53-foot Dry Van equipment.
Single Truck Limit Eligible carriers may enroll exactly one (1) approved power unit under trial evaluation terms. Additional units follow standard plan schedules.
Explicit Exclusions Carriers entering via referral programs or enrolling under Flat Rate weekly fee structures are not eligible for the 14-day evaluation program.
Owner Administrative Approval Service does not begin automatically upon application. All carrier authority, safety scores, and insurance certificates must be manually approved by our compliance desk.

How the 14-Day Evaluation Functions

The 14-calendar-day period begins on the confirmed administrative start date following full document execution. During this period:

  • The carrier retains 100% decision authority on all proposed loads.
  • All broker rate confirmations are reviewed and executed directly by the motor carrier.
  • Carrier is responsible for all operating expenses, fuel, insurance, maintenance, and accessorials.
  • No service fees are charged for dispatch coordination during the approved trial window.
  • At trial conclusion, continued dispatch service requires explicit mutual execution of ongoing percentage terms. No automatic card charges or silent roll-overs.
