Referral rules · held for review

Referral priority is not a service guarantee.

A carrier referred by a valid code is intended to receive priority at the front of the waitlist after eligibility, administrative, and capacity review. Priority does not guarantee acceptance, a start date, a load, or a service level.

Intended reward conditions

  • The referred carrier does not receive the ordinary 14-day trial.
  • A referrer earns 14 days of credit per eligible referred truck only after the first complete qualifying invoice is paid and reconciled.
  • The owner has directed a seven-calendar-day code-entry window, a 14-day objection window, and a 180-day credit-expiry framework.
  • Valid earned rewards may accumulate without a predefined numerical cap; adjustments and reversals must be traceable.

Schedule B is not fully aligned. Attribution, expiry start date, disputes, reversals, credit allocation, and related administration need to be reconciled and reviewed. This candidate has no live referral-code validation, carrier accounts, reward ledger, or credit application. No balances or actual rewards are displayed.
