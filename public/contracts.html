Master Carrier Dispatch Services Agreement

MASTER CONTRACT

1. Parties and Relationship

This Carrier Dispatch Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made by and between AHMED ABDULHAMID OMAR ALMAJDOUB, doing business as Almajdoub Dispatch (“Provider”), with primary notice email contact@almajdoubdispatch.com and direct operations phone +1 (409) 941-7180, and the authorized motor carrier identified in the signature section below (“Carrier”). Carrier retains Provider for administrative dispatch and back-office support for Carrier’s own motor-carrier business under Carrier-specific instructions.

2. Scope of Administrative Services

Subject to Carrier’s instructions, capacity, and the applicable written plan, Provider may:

  1. Search for potential freight for Carrier’s specifically identified truck using authorized load boards and industry sources.
  2. Contact licensed freight brokers about loads identified for this Carrier; request load details and negotiate within Carrier’s written rate and lane limits.
  3. Help organize Carrier-provided broker setup documents and load paperwork through secure, approved channels.
  4. Communicate pickup and delivery appointment details and relay status/check-call information supplied by Carrier or driver.
  5. Help organize time-stamped facility arrival and departure evidence for Carrier’s review of potential detention ($50/hr) or accessorial requests.
Service Boundaries: Provider does not promise a load, rate, revenue, miles, detention payment, layover, TONU, claim outcome, or continuous location monitoring. Provider is not an insurer or guarantor of broker solvency.

3. Carrier Sole Authority & Zero Forced Dispatch

Carrier retains the sole and final decision to accept or reject each load, rate, route, appointment, and material change. Provider may not accept or book a load, sign a Rate Confirmation, make a transportation commitment, or bind Carrier without Carrier’s specific, recorded approval for that individual load.

The transportation agreement and Rate Confirmation must name the authorized Carrier as the motor carrier. Provider is not a party to the transportation contract and may not reassign, re-broker, or double-broker any shipment.

4. Multi-Carrier Operating Boundary

Provider maintains separate carrier profiles and written instructions for every carrier. A load search or broker communication begins exclusively for a named Carrier and its specifically identified truck. Provider does not allocate or rotate freight opportunities based on internal discretion or pricing preference.

5. Fees, Invoicing, and Payment Schedule

Carrier selects its commercial plan under Schedule A. No card or bank account is automatically debited.

  • Service Week: Monday 00:00 through Sunday 23:59 `America/Chicago`.
  • Invoice Issuance: Friday at 12:00 noon `America/Chicago`, prior to factoring distributions.
  • Payment Due: Friday evening. Service continues through Monday morning; unpaid accounts may be suspended at the Monday suspension point.
  • Started Week Rule: Flat-rate fees prepay the upcoming service week. Once a service week starts, the full weekly rate for active trucks in that week is due.

6. Records, Privacy, and Data Protection

Provider uses carrier information solely to perform dispatch services and maintain operations. Provider does not sell carrier data or transmit documents to unauthorized external parties. Track 1 operations are human-directed; no tax or carrier documents are submitted to unverified third-party models.

7. Term and Month-to-Month Termination

This Agreement begins upon electronic execution and continues on a month-to-month basis. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time with written notice. Undisputed fees for started service weeks remain payable; no long-term lock-in or cancellation penalty applies.

Schedule A — Pricing and Billing Schedule

Counsel-Reviewed Schedule · Governs Linehaul Commissions & Flat Rates
SCHEDULE A

Commercial Plan Options — 53-Foot Dry Van

Carrier elects one commercial compensation model below for each active power unit:

  • Standard Percentage Plan: 7% of gross Linehaul per load booked and completed. Applies to single-truck owner-operators and small fleets.
  • Founder Rate Lock Plan: 5% of gross Linehaul for qualifying enrollments while the launch gate is open (capped at 5 verified first-paid carrier conversions).
  • Flat-Rate Fleet Plan: $299 per truck per week, minimum three (3) trucks (prepaid Friday for upcoming Monday–Sunday service week). Best suited for small fleets operating 3 or more trucks.
  • Founding Flat-Rate Plan: $245 per truck per week, minimum two (2) trucks (confirmed in writing for eligible founding seats).

Direct 14-Calendar-Day Commission-Free Trial

One directly registered, non-referred Carrier may receive one 14-calendar-day commission-free trial for one accepted 53' Dry Van truck on the percentage plan. The trial period begins upon administrative approval. Zero automatic rollover: trial expiration does not create a paid plan or authorize any card/bank charge. Continued service requires Carrier’s affirmative selection and written acceptance of an ongoing paid plan.

100% Accessorial & Detention Pass-Through

The dispatch commission applies strictly to the separately identified Linehaul rate. Separately itemized accessorials—including Detention ($50/hour standard), TONU (Truck Ordered Not Used), Layover, Driver Assist, and Stop-off charges—are 100% passed through to Carrier with 0% deducted by Provider.

Billing Mechanics & Settlement

Invoices are delivered electronically at 12:00 noon `America/Chicago` on Fridays before factoring proceeds are distributed. Payment is due Friday evening; service continues through Monday morning. Payment methods include ACH Wire Transfer, Direct Deposit, or Payoneer. Unearned future flat-rate weeks are refunded upon timely written notice before the service week begins.

Schedule B — Carrier Referral Program Terms

Counsel-Reviewed Terms · Incentives & Priority Queue
SCHEDULE B

1. Referral Queue Priority

A motor carrier onboarded using a verified referral code bypasses the general waitlist and receives priority review at the front of the intake queue. Priority does not waive authority or insurance compliance checks.

2. Referral Credit Rules (14-Day Free Dispatch)

When an existing Carrier refers a new active motor carrier whose first weekly dispatch invoice is paid in full and reconciled, the referring Carrier earns 14 consecutive calendar days with no dispatch commission on one designated truck (worth up to ~$700+ in dispatch commission savings based on typical linehaul volume). For flat-rate fleets, the credit allocates as two 7-day truck-week units. Credits are applied sequentially against future weekly invoices and possess no independent cash resale value.

Carrier-Specific Limited Dispatch Authorization

AUTHORIZATION

Scope of Limited Authority

Carrier authorizes Provider solely to:

  1. Contact licensed freight brokers to request load details and discuss rates and operating terms within Carrier’s written parameters.
  2. Transmit Carrier-approved carrier setup documents (W-9, COI, Authority Certificate) through broker-authorized secure channels.
  3. Relay appointment and check-call status information supplied by Carrier, driver, or broker.

Strict Prohibitions

Provider may NOT accept or reject a load, sign a Rate Confirmation, bind Carrier to a transportation contract, change Carrier’s insurance, endorse or receive payment, access Carrier’s bank accounts, or present itself as the motor carrier. Carrier must individually approve every load in a recorded communication before any commitment is made.

ADDENDUM

Refrigerated Freight Terms & Scope

Refrigeration & Temperature Disclaimer: Provider does not operate, set, inspect, monitor, or control the refrigeration unit or temperature recorder.
