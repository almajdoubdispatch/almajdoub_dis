Founder Waitlist

Your truck. Your call.

Submit once to request a founder-seat eligibility review. Eligibility is confirmed after authority identity review; joining the list is not carrier approval, a signed service agreement, or a start-of-service confirmation.

How the Founder Waitlist works

  • If identity review confirms eligibility, the record preserves the original submission time.
  • The list closes after five distinct carriers complete an eligible first invoice that is paid and reconciled; there is no calendar deadline.
  • Several trucks or repeated payments from one carrier count as one carrier.
  • Every carrier and requested truck remains subject to manual review and actual operating capacity.
