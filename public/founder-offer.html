Frequently Asked Questions

Does joining the Founder Waitlist obligate me to haul?

No. Registration simply records your interest and time-stamp for founder pricing eligibility. Service begins only after mutual review, carrier approval, signed agreement, and your explicit load acceptance.

What equipment qualifies for the Founder offer?

Our core operational focus is 53-foot Dry Van trailers. 53-foot Reefer equipment is also eligible on a secondary basis, subject to a signed Reefer amendment, carrier authorization, and per-truck capacity review.

How does Almajdoub Dispatch bill for services?

Dispatch service fees are earned and invoiced weekly only after loads are delivered and acceptable Proof of Delivery (POD) is provided. We operate on America/Chicago billing schedules.
